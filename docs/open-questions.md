# Open Questions

These are items the reverse-engineering evidence did not settle. Each one has a fallback in this project.
All fallbacks are configurable in `ReviewConfig` (backend `review-core`) unless noted otherwise.

| # | Question | Evidence so far | Fallback used | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engine and depth used by the cloud review for each strength preset (Fast/Standard/Deep/Maximum) | Client sends `GameReviewSearchSettings(Strength)` and SF 16.1 NNUE or Torch. Local feedback uses depth 18. The Chess.com settings page lists Game Review engines `stockfish16 nnue` and `torch-human` and strengths Fast/Standard/Deep/Maximum as depths 18/22/24/26. | Stockfish (any version) with presets 12/18/22/26 plies or a time budget. Calibration ran at depth 18 against the app's Fast. **Resolved in part (2026-10-18):** captured reviews show the "Stockfish 16" setting is scored by Stockfish 18 with a node/time budget (fresh searches reach depth 12-23, mean 17.4) and principal-variation reuse; the default engine is Torch. See `docs/calibration/chesscom-review-protocol.md`. | Scores near thresholds can differ; our accuracy is about +2.8 higher (see `docs/parity.md`) |
