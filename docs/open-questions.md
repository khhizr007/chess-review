# Open Questions

These are items the reverse-engineering evidence did not settle. Each one has a fallback in this project.
All fallbacks are configurable in `ReviewConfig` (backend `review-core`) unless noted otherwise.

| # | Question | Evidence so far | Fallback used | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engine and depth used by the cloud review for each strength preset (Fast/Standard/Deep/Maximum) | Client sends `GameReviewSearchSettings(Strength)` and SF 16.1 NNUE or Torch. Local feedback uses depth 18. The Chess.com settings page lists Game Review engines `stockfish16 nnue` and `torch-human` and strengths Fast/Standard/Deep/Maximum as depths 18/22/24/26. | Stockfish (any version) with presets 12/18/22/26 plies or a time budget. Calibration ran at depth 18 against the app's Fast. | Scores near thresholds can differ; our accuracy is about +2.8 higher (see `docs/parity.md`) |
| 2 | Exact `is_forced` definition | Name only | One legal move | Rare differences |
| 3 | Opening-book contents and the "book" rule | Embedded proprietary book (`init_embedded_book`) | Public-domain Lichess `chess-openings` positions: a move is Book while the resulting position is in that set and every earlier move was Book | Book length can differ |
| 4 | Sacrifice definition behind Brilliant (`node_lib::is_sacrifice`, base tags 24/25/209) | Gates recovered: material vs piece value, endgame gain ≥ 200, opponent EP ≥ 0.1. Calibration: the app gave 4 Brilliants in 20 games (1,175 moves), one of them Legal's trap, where the piece given up is the queen left en prise, not the moved knight. | Static exchange test on the moved piece, played move = engine best, second-best clearly worse (≥ 0.10 EP), EP window; see `docs/implementation-notes.md`. Tuning set: 1 of 3 found, no false ones (was 60 false). Counting pieces left en prise is available but off. | Brilliant is now rare and precise; too few app Brilliants to tune finely |
| 5 | Great Find's difficulty model and human move-probability model | Calls to `PositionDifficultyRegistry` and `move_probability` (ML models). Calibration: every app Great move was our engine's best move, but the gap to the second line separates them poorly, and a shallow-search proxy (depth 4–8) finds nearly all of them. | MultiPV gap ≥ 0.20 EP plus the recovered exclusions. Tuning set: precision 0.57, recall 0.15. A gap of 0.13 did better on the tuning games but not on the holdout games. | Great Find is under-reported (about 1 in 6 found) |
| 6 | Tricky classification | Requires the ML models above. The Android client drops it. | Disabled | None in the UI |
| 7 | Detectors for the six Miss subtypes (free piece, tactic, win, comeback, brilliant, opportunity) | Decision order and A/B guards confirmed. "Miss" itself is still gated by the confirmed guards. |
